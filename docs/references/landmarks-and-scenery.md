# Landmarks and scenery

Buildings, structures and surroundings: pit complex and control tower, the pit-straight scoring tower, grandstands, the pedestrian walkway bridges, campgrounds at McPhillamy Park and Reid/Sulman Park, houses along Mountain and Conrod Straights, the National Motor Racing Museum, the Wahluu/Skyline boardwalk and lookout, barriers and tyre walls, catch/debris fencing, vegetation and wildlife, seasons and weather, and the town of Bathurst. Many entries are OpenStreetMap objects (grandstands, campgrounds, bridges, tunnels, boardwalk) that give real positions for placing props. Kerb colours and wall paint are best read from the photo hubs at the end of the file.

Descriptions of photos and videos are written from page titles, captions and metadata; the media itself was not downloaded, so check the framing before relying on a specific angle. Every URL was fetched or came from a search result when this library was compiled (2026-10-07); a few sites (for example artstation.com, bathurst.nsw.gov.au, cgtrader.com, fandom.com) refuse automated requests with 403 or 202 responses but did appear in search results.

**Reference count: 101**
