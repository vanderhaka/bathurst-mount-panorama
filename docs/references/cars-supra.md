# Cars: Gen3 Toyota GR Supra

References for the 2026 Toyota GR Supra Gen3 Supercar (Walkinshaw Andretti United / Walkinshaw TWG Racing and Brad Jones Racing): the 2024 clay model, the 2025 launch car, the January 2026 race-spec renders, freely licensed photos of the car on parade and on track, and the A90 road car the body is derived from. Reported facts to cross-check: same Gen3 control chassis and wheelbase as the Camaro and Mustang, a 5.2 litre quad-cam V8 based on the Lexus 2UR-GSE, a "boat nose" inspired by the 2014 FT-1 concept, wide flares, splitter, diffuser and the Gen3 rear wing. No overall race-car height is published; the road A90 is 1,290 to 1,295 mm tall, so the game uses 1.23 m (road height less the ~65 mm used for the other two cars in docs/research/car-specs.md).

What was actually seen: the pages were rendered headless with scripts/shot.mjs and the screenshots read back. Entries marked **(seen)** are ones whose photo or render was legible in those screenshots and was used to shape the model: the three Adelaide parade photos and the Bathurst test photo on Commons, the two race-spec renders on Touring Car Times, and four A90 road and GT4 photos on Commons (side, rear and rear three-quarter). Every other description comes from page titles, captions and search summaries. Speedcafe pages timed out in the headless browser. Searches for Motorsport.com and Drive coverage of the reveal returned no matching articles, so none are listed. Every URL was fetched or came from a search result when this library was compiled (2026-10-07).
