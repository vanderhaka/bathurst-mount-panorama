# Cars: 1979 HDT Holden Torana A9X #05

Visual reference for the game's tribute car: Peter Brock's Holden Dealer Team LX Torana SS A9X hatchback, #05, winner of the 1979 Hardie-Ferodo 1000 with Jim Richards. Physical data is in docs/research/car-specs.md section 12.

How this library was made (2026-10-08): a research agent viewed the Wikimedia Commons photos below in a browser and wrote the descriptions from them. Most large photos show Brock-livery display cars (Bowden's Own collection, 2009-2011). V8 Sleuth says that the 1978 and 1979 winners were near-identical separate cars, so a photo may show either one. The small Bathurst-museum photo (`Brock-a9x-79.jpg`) is the confirmed 1979 winner and agrees with the others on every part it shows. Colours below are estimates from photos.

**The game uses no real logos.** Every sponsor word or logo (Marlboro, HDT, Holden, Castrol, Bridgestone, radio stations) becomes a plain colour block. The colour zones, stripes and chevrons stay.

## 2. Body shape

- **Proportions:** a long bonnet and a short deck on a two-door fastback hatchback. Length 4.51 m, wheelbase 2.586 m.
- **Bonnet:** long (about 1.1-1.2 m from cowl to nose). A bolt-on box hump runs forward from the windscreen base over about 45-50 % of the bonnet: about 0.6 m wide (about 40 % of the bonnet), 6-9 cm tall at the rear, with a flat top, steep side walls with tight edges, a short rounded ramp at the front and a row of rivets around its base (`Brock-a9x-79.jpg`, `Holden_Torana_LX_SS_A9X_Hatchback.jpg`). Its **rear face is the scoop opening**, at the windscreen base; no photo shows it from behind, so its height is estimated. Two bonnet pins near the front corners, about ±0.3 m from the centre line.
- **Front:** two round headlamps (one each side) in black rounded-rectangle recesses. Between them, a full-width black egg-crate grille with horizontal bars. A body-colour header panel above the grille. The race car has a fibreglass nose with an integral bumper and a deep **air dam** in body colour, brake-duct slots at the lower corners and a black chin lip. Headlamps are often taped.
- **Roofline:** a flat roof to about the B-pillar, then a fastback slope of about 25-30 degrees to a large raked rear glass. A thick C-pillar. A long, low, tapered quarter window behind the door glass. Black window surrounds.
- **Doors:** two, with framed glass. A small black rectangular flag mirror at the front upper corner of each door. Behind the door glass, a black louvred vent of 5-6 vertical slats (about 0.16 m long) fills the gap to the quarter window.
- **Rear:** a three-piece fibreglass **spoiler**: a ducktail across the full tailgate width plus a bolted end cap at each corner (a visible seam, two bolts). The caps rise about 5-8 cm above the centre lip and wrap down the rear quarter as a triangle above the tail lamp. Flat horizontal rectangular tail lamps at each end of the rear panel, in a black bezel, crossed by one black bar just above mid-height: from the inboard end a narrow red cell, the main red section, then amber over the outboard 40-45 % (`Holden_Torana_A9X_HDT_(14865995080).jpg`, `(15052284712)`). A painted steel bumper. A round quick-release fuel cap in the centre of the rear panel. A black rubber mudflap board hangs below the bumper.
- **Wheel arches:** bolt-on fibreglass flares front and rear, about 50-70 mm proud, ending in a thick rolled lip about 40 mm wide and cut square at the foot; a black joint line where the flare meets the body; 6-7 dome bolts on each front flare (`Holden_Torana_LX_A9X_Brock_(14809398215).jpg`).
- **Exhaust:** a side exit on the left (passenger) side, under the sill just ahead of the rear wheel; oval, about 100 mm wide.
- **Roll cage:** visible through the quarter windows and the rear glass.

## 4. Livery: "White 05" (slot 2) and the other slots

Slot 2 is a white car with red and black stripes in the style of the Holden Dealer Team cars of the early 1970s, with "05" in black. It does not copy the 1979 sponsor colour design. Slots 3 and 4 are fictional period-style schemes in the same spirit as the other cars' fictional liveries.

## 5. Wheels

Five studs, a flat dark hub disc, about 10 slim spokes in five V pairs, a black or dark-grey face and a polished deep-dish outer lip. Size about 15 x 10 inches. (A forum says HDT used magnesium Jongbloed wheels in 1979; this is not confirmed.)

## 6. Cockpit

Right-hand drive. The road A9X dash (`MMWA_1979_Holden_Torana_A9X-Dashboard.jpg`) has three equal hooded round dials in a row on a silver face; from the driver's view, left to right: a combination gauge, the speedometer and the tachometer. Two small round gauges sit low on the dash to the right of the steering column. A three-spoke wheel with a T-shaped lower spoke drilled with two holes. A silver-grey dash face with a black surround. The race car had a large-diameter wheel and no power steering; a floor-mounted gear lever on the tunnel with a long chrome lever and a white ball knob; a bucket seat; fawn interior trim on the 1979 winner. No photo of the Group C cockpit was found, so the game uses the road-car layout.
