# Cars: Gen3 Camaro ZL1 and Mustang GT

References for the two Gen3 Supercars: technical and regulation articles, reveal coverage, Wikimedia Commons photos (race cars on track and on parade, plus road-car references for body shape: Camaro ZL1 sixth generation and Mustang S650), cockpit, steering wheel and MoTeC display material, onboards, engine sound recordings and walk-arounds. Reported Gen3 figures to cross-check before hard-coding: wheelbase about 2765 mm, 18 x 11 inch wheels, Dunlop control tyre, V8 around 600 hp (Camaro 5.7 litre, Mustang Coyote-based). Where a source says Reid Park it may mean the Townsville street circuit rather than Bathurst.

Descriptions of photos and videos are written from page titles, captions and metadata; the media itself was not downloaded, so check the framing before relying on a specific angle. Every URL was fetched or came from a search result when this library was compiled (2026-10-07); a few sites (for example artstation.com, bathurst.nsw.gov.au, cgtrader.com, fandom.com) refuse automated requests with 403 or 202 responses but did appear in search results.
